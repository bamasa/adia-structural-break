## (a) Data generation — what the organisers actually say

### 2025 edition (break always at the known boundary, one score per series)
- **[quote]** "The training data combines a large collection (around 10,000 series) of synthetic time series with known break labels, exhibiting a wide variety of break types - including changes in mean, variance, distributional shape, dependence structure, and tail behaviour." — https://hub.crunchdao.com/competitions/structural-break and https://docs.crunchdao.com/competitions/competitions/adia-lab-structural-break-challenge
- **[quote]** "The dataset for this competition comprises tens of thousands of synthetic univariate time series, each containing approximately 1,000 to 5,000 values with a designated boundary point." … "designed to represent various real-world scenarios … financial markets, climate data, industrial sensor readings, and biomedical signals" (same page).
- **[quote]** Definition section: "consider a random walk characterized by parameters such as drift μ and volatility σ. A structural break is said to occur at the moment one or more of these parameters experience a change - for example, volatility σ changing from 1.0 to 2.0"; also "constructing a single time series by combining segments with inherently different behaviors or data sources - for instance … switching to a different thermometer"; "This change can be either abrupt or smooth and could manifest as parameter changes, functional form changes, regime transitions, or combinations thereof." (same page).
- **[quote]** Data-format note: "The structural break occurs at the change in value, but it may take some time (values) to become detectable/apparent." (same page).
- Organiser on the forum, asked how ground truth is generated: **[quote]** "All of the information we have been provided by the client are on the overview page." — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/structural-break-model-validation/986. ADIA Lab (relayed by staff) on whether the task is "distribution D_m vs D_n with parameters": **[quote]** "Yes, that could be one possible angle, even though it does not take into account the time series structure as well as many other components." — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/so-the-task-is-about-detection-not-prediction/971
- Crunch Lab's post-competition LinkedIn post describes the test bed as **[quote]** "stochastic processes" and "real-world signals such as stock returns, ECG traces, or temperature records" — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/crunchlabhq_were-proud-to-see-our-collaboration-with-activity-7389652138681061376-VXp- (so the private test contained real series even though the training set was described as synthetic).
- No arXiv paper / technical report describing the generator was found (searches on arXiv, ADIA Lab site, CrunchDAO blog). The only "generator fingerprints" are participant-discovered:
  - **[participant claim, 4th place]** the global coefficient of variation gates three regimes: **[quote]** "LightGBM splits on global_cv_var at a small number of thresholds (≈0.03–0.04 and ≈0.20)" … "That narrow bucket (0.198, 0.20] appears to be an 'easy negatives' regime: extremely low positive rate (~1%) … makes up nearly ~17% of all samples"; "the average 'global_cv' of all False samples is 5.5 and the average for all True samples is 19.5" — https://angry-sandal-e9b.notion.site/ADIA-Lab-2025-Structural-Break-286f5ef98def808a8a2ceb0c017e30a7
  - **[participant claim, 5th place]** the decisive interaction was `std²/mean` of the whole raw series ("magic") — https://messy-plain-a65.notion.site/ADIA25-211402a1a1b780428471eaed714e285b
  - **[inferred]** these are raw-scale artefacts of the 2025 generator; they cannot transfer to the Real-Time Edition because all RT series are z-scored on the historical segment (see below).

### 2026 Real-Time Edition
- **[quote]** "The training data (with known structural break locations) combines a large collection of synthetic and real-world time series exhibiting a wide variety of break types - including changes in mean, variance, distributional shape, and dependence structure." — https://hub.crunchdao.com/competitions/structural-break-real-time (also docs .md: https://docs.crunchdao.com/competitions/competitions/structural-break-real-time.md)
- **[quote]** "The dataset contains a large and diverse collection of univariate time series exhibiting many different kinds of structural breaks -- changes in mean, variance, distribution shape, correlation structure, and more. All series are pre-processed into a common z-scored format." (hub overview, Dataset section)
- **[quote]** "historical segment … typically between 1,000 and 5,000 observations … guaranteed to contain no break"; "online segment … typically between 10 and 1,000 observations long"; "Each series contains at most one structural break"; "With probability 0.5, a break occurs at some unknown point within the online segment … With probability 0.5, no break occurs"; "the break position tau is a 0-indexed position within the online segment at which the break occurs, or None" (hub overview / docs).
- Hub FAQ (accordion, expanded in the browser), all **[quote]**:
  - "What data is used to compute the mean and standard deviation for standardizing each series?" → "Only the historical (reference) segment, not the online period or the full series."
  - "Is standardization done separately for each series?" → "Yes, each series is standardized independently."
  - "Are the normalization parameters (mean/std) updated once the online period starts?" → "No. They are fixed before the online stream begins and never updated afterward."
  - "Are real and synthetic series normalized the same way?" → "Yes, both go through the same standardization pipeline."
  - "Is the reference window assumed to be free of structural breaks or irregularities?" → "Yes and no: there are no structural breaks by definition (the reference window defines what is 'normal'), but there can be irregularities (jumps, etc.)."
  - "Is it possible to use the dataset from the previous Structural Break competition?" → "Yes, but it is not officially encouraged. The dataset was designed for another type of problem, which does not match this year's objective of finding not IF, but WHEN there is a structural break."
- Data fix: **[quote]** "Fixed 29 bad timeseries that reported no structural breaks when one was actually present in the very first step." — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/2026-w23-structural-break-fixes/1156 (2026-06-08). **[inferred]** τ = 0 exists, and label_t = 1 iff t ≥ τ (0-indexed), consistent with "positive at step t if the break has already occurred by that step".
- Splits **[quote]**: public training 10,000 (local & cloud); public reduced test 100 (local); public test 10,000 (cloud); private test 10,000 (cloud). For 2025 the staff confirmed the 100 local series are part of the 10,000 cloud public set (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/out-of-sample-data-in-structural-breaks-competition/990); **[inferred]** same design in 2026.
- Nothing public on magnitude distributions, process families, or τ priors. Participant measurements only:
  - **[participant claim]** break-type mix measured by an oracle on the training set: "dependence 54%, volatility 24%, tails 11%, mean 8%, variance 3%" — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/where-does-the-remaining-headroom-live-sharing-measured-ceilings-from-a-whitening-based-approach/1206 (post #5).
  - **[participant claim]** "The length of the online segment appears unpredictable from the historical segment (negative out-of-fold R² across 23 descriptors)" (same thread, post #1).
  - **[participant claim, low-scoring repo]** "small online segments (median ~500 points) and uniformly distributed break points τ" — https://github.com/rahulm2706/ADIA-Lab-Structural-Break-Challenge-Real-Time-Edition
  - **[participant claim]** between-series vs within-series drift: "between-series sd 0.332 vs within-series sd 0.116; benign history→online drift of 0.307 exceeds the median break of 0.194" (thread 1206, post #3).

## (b) Protocol, metric, runner — verified from source

Runner and scorer are public and identical in both organiser repos: `competitions/structural-break-real-time/scoring/{runner.py,scoring.py}` in https://github.com/crunchdao/competitions and https://github.com/crunchdao/quickstarters (local copies: `scratchpad/research/repos/…`). crunch-cli (https://github.com/crunchdao/crunch-cli) only hosts the generic "unstructured" runner context; the competition-specific runner is downloaded from the competitions repo (`crunch/runner/cloud.py` sets `runner_dot_py_file_path = scoring_directory/runner.py`).

What `infer` receives — **[from runner.py]**:
- `datasets` is a Python generator (`dataset_stream`) yielding `(historical, online_generator)`; `historical` is a **numpy float32 array** (sent over a socket as `historical.astype(numpy.float32).tobytes()`); `x_online` is a **true generator** (`online_stream`) with **no length**, one float32 value per `ONLINE_POINT` packet. Protocol errors (verbatim messages): "yield must be called once before iterating over datasets", "previous value not yield-ed", "multiple yield detected", "yield not called enough times", "code did not exit after last dataset", "cannot iterate in online stream in a child process after a fork".
- The prediction is post-processed by `_post_process_infer_yield_result` (float/int/bool, or 1-element list/Series/ndarray) and sent back **as numpy.float32** (`RemoteCommand.NEW_PREDICTION` encodes `numpy.float32(value)`), so **scores are quantised to float32 before scoring**. The code does not enforce the [0,1] range (the rules do).
- Cloud uses `X_test.parquet`, local uses `X_test.reduced.parquet`; series are grouped by `id` (sorted); `_split_periods` uses `period == 1` for historical and `period == 2` for online.
- Determinism check: `percentage = 0.1`, `tolerance = 1e-8`, rerun on `datasets[:int(0.1*len)]` (the first 10 % in id order), compared with `numpy.allclose(..., atol=1e-8)`.
- Parallelism: `INFER_PARALLELISM` read from the module globals; series split into contiguous batches (`_split_into_batches`); each worker is a forked process; `0` = all CPUs; capped at `os.cpu_count()`; requires crunch-cli ≥ 11.7.0.
- Commit history of the runner (https://github.com/crunchdao/competitions/commits/master/competitions/structural-break-real-time): added 2026-05-01; metric renamed 05-03; "use server-client to provide infer data to executor" 06-03 (the leak fix); parallelism 06-11; determinism check reduced to 10 % 06-12; version-parse fix 07-21; pre-trained-models section 09-07. No change to `scoring.py` after 05-03.

Metric — **[from scoring.py]**, matches the docs formula:
```
merged["time_online"] = merged.groupby("id").cumcount()          # 0,1,2,... per series
for time, group in merged.groupby("time_online"):
    n_pos, n_neg = labels.sum(), (1-labels).sum()
    if n_pos == 0 or n_neg == 0: continue                          # step contributes nothing
    weighted_auc_sum += n_pos*n_neg * roc_auc_score(labels, scores)
    total_weight     += n_pos*n_neg
score = weighted_auc_sum/total_weight   (0.5 if total_weight == 0)
```
- `y_test` has one `target` row per (id, time) of the online segment; **[inferred]** "series alive at step t" = series whose online segment has length > t; nothing else is truncated or capped.
- **[quote]** "The ideal score is a step function: it stays at 0 as long as no break has occurred, then jumps to 1 as soon as the break happens." "TS-AUC = 0.5 … is exactly what you get if your score depends only on t" (baseline notebook / docs).
- **[inferred from the formula]** weight n_pos(t)·n_neg(t) is small at early t (few positives) and shrinks late as series end; a participant states "long ages carry most of the metric weight" (thread 1206). The public 0.5-baseline statement means any per-step global monotone transform of scores is harmless; only cross-series ordering at the same t matters.

Rules and operations:
- Time limit **[quote]** "15 hours per week" (quota refresh Wednesdays 16:00 UTC — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/structural-break-quote-refresh-time/1172); run timeout is quota + 30 min (https://docs.crunchdao.com/competitions/participate/resources-limit.md). Run creation offers a "Powerful" instance option (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/structural-break-real-time-submit-pretrained-model/1178); machine specs are not public.
- Deadline: extended by two weeks (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/2026-w36-background-work/1207) → **October 1st, 2026 at 4:00 p.m. UTC**; final evaluation end of October; winners at ADIA Lab Symposium 26–28 Oct (hub overview).
- Out-of-sample: **[quote]** "your model will only be run once on a brand new dataset" (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/structural-break-real-time-may-infer-use-prior-completed-series-state/1186). In 2025 the last selected model was used if none was chosen (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/september-2025-highlights-challenges-growth-updates/1049).
- Leaderboard comparability: all predictions made before the June-8 leak fix were invalidated — **[quote]** "All predictions were invalidated. … The leaderboard doesn't consider them at all." The leak was the online length obtained by memory exploration; the fix moved data delivery to a socket, "+3 minutes" per run — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/leaderboard-comparability-after-the-june-8-real-time-data-access-fix-were-pre-fix-scores-rescored/1188. So today's top scores are post-fix and comparable to ours.
- Online length: **[quote]** "The length of the x_online object is not accessible by design." — https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/structural-break-real-time-is-any-legal-online-horizon-length-metadata-available-at-serve/1180
- Cross-series state: mechanically possible, but **[quote]** "trying to persist state across time series will likely result in your code not being deterministic" because parallel partitions and the 10 % determinism rerun start at different offsets (thread 1186, 1188). Effectively: keep series independent.
- Pre-trained models: allowed only if the training code is included (hub overview "Pre-trained models"); TSFMs allowed but must be bundled, no internet (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/using-tsfm-time-series-foundation-models-for-structural-break-contest/939).
- Sharing: write-ups encouraged, data may not be redistributed (https://forum.crunchdao.com/t/sharing-finding/1095). Solutions publication after the deadline is voluntary (thread 1206, staff post).

## (d) Ranked ideas (expected gain = my estimate, not a source figure, unless a source delta is quoted)

1. **Upgrade the whitening stream: AR(p≤12) by BIC + conditional-scale (EWMA/GARCH-type) + probability-integral transform, fitted on the history only; run the existing detector battery on it.** Both documented RT approaches above 0.61 are built on this ("Running the same statistics on the raw series instead of the whitened one is about 2.5× worse, and the gap widens at longer ages" — thread 1206), one participant's oracle puts dependence + volatility breaks at ~78 % of positives, and our whitening is AR(1). Expected gain +0.005–0.015 (estimate; the two forum authors reached 0.62–0.63 with this as the backbone). Effort: medium (feature recompute + retrain); inference cost small (p ≤ 12 recursion).
2. **Per-series null calibration of channels** (express each streaming statistic as a quantile/p-value against the null distribution simulated from the fitted history model, or block-bootstrapped from the history). Cross-sectional AUC compares series with very different noise structure; the 0.6263 solution has an explicit "calib (5): per-series null variance" block and the 0.6195 one scores every cell "quantile-scored against a historical null". Expected +0.003–0.01 (estimate). Effort: medium; watch the 15 h/week budget (use `INFER_PARALLELISM`).
3. **Shiryaev–Roberts / Bayes-factor channels integrated over τ and amplitude for mean, scale and dependence** (closed-form conjugate marginals over 8–16 window lengths), alongside our CUSUM (max over τ) and BOCPD. Source: "bayesv12 (12): Bayes factors integrated over τ (16 window lengths) and over break amplitude; 3 channels (mean, scale, linear dependence)" (thread 1206). Expected +0.002–0.006 (estimate). Effort: low–medium.
4. **Exact-scorer hygiene, cheap and verifiable:** (i) predictions are cast to float32 by the runner — check our score transform never saturates (values ≥ 1−6e-8 become exact 1.0 ties; ties count ½ in AUC); keep scores in an unsaturated monotone range (e.g. squash to [0.001, 0.999] or use a wide logit range) — a global monotone transform cannot change TS-AUC otherwise; (ii) cast historical/online inputs to float32 in local validation to mimic the cloud; (iii) confirm our training labels use label_t = 1[t ≥ τ] (τ 0-indexed; W23 fix says a break "present in the very first step" is positive at step 0); (iv) weight training steps by n_pos(t)·n_neg(t) of the training pool if not already. Expected 0 to +0.003; effort: hours.
5. **Dual reference frames: recent tail of the history vs whole history.** The FAQ states the reference window can contain "irregularities (jumps, etc.)", one participant measured benign history→online drift larger than the median break, and one repo reports "+0.025 CV TS-AUC" from history-tail-vs-whole descriptors (low absolute level, so treat as directional). Add channels computed against the last 256/512 history points and history-drift descriptors as static features. Expected +0.002–0.008 (estimate). Effort: low.
6. **History-family descriptors as static conditioning features** (history length, kurtosis, ACF profile, fitted AR order/persistence, GARCH persistence, jump count, value-grid statistics such as unique-value ratio / minimum gap that separate real quantised signals from synthetic ones). The 0.6263 solution has a "histx (16): history descriptors × current scale statistic" block; the 2025 winners all exploited generator regimes via global descriptors. Expected +0.002–0.006 (estimate; speculative on the real/synthetic split). Effort: low.
7. **Add the 2025-style distribution-shape tests on whitened innovations vs the historical innovation ECDF** (KS/CvM/AD at several thresholds, max over dyadic windows; central-slab JSD; near-zero occupancy / ring shares; tail ES deltas). These are the families common to the 1st/4th/6th 2025 solutions and to the 0.6263 RT "rosenl" block, and we lack the shape-only variants. Expected +0.001–0.005 (estimate; the forum warns that breadth alone saturates quickly). Effort: low.
8. **Post-processing the score path (running max / decayed max) and light stacking of members with strictly disjoint-half validation.** Several RT repos apply "monotone score adjustments"; the metric only rewards keeping detected positives above negatives at later t.
