## The six ideas

- **sr_cond** — Shiryaev-Roberts odds on the CONDITIONAL normal-score stream for the mean and lag-1 dependence families (12 channels), odds for lag-2 and lag-3 dependence on the unconditional stream (8 channels), and a ONE-SIDED variance-increase CUSUM (1 channel); 21 new channels for all rows.
- **mlp** — a per-step multilayer perceptron (119 → 256 → 128 → 1, GELU, dropout 0.1) as a second learner; loss = BCE + the per-step pairwise ranking loss; AdamW lr 1e-3, batches of 4096 rows grouped by step, 3 epochs; torch on MPS allowed for this idea only.
- **ar12** — the AR order fixed at 12, and chosen by AIC instead of BIC; each variant measured as a replacement of WHITE90.
- **tpit** — a fitted Student-t CDF instead of the empirical CDF in the probability integral transform (df by maximum likelihood over a grid, scale re-estimated per df).
- **robust_ar** — AR coefficients by iteratively reweighted least squares with Huber weights (threshold 2.5, 5 iterations), innovation scale by 1.4826·MAD; also report the alone-AUC on the fold-2 series whose largest |z| exceeds 8.
- **early** — short-window versions of the strongest statistics for steps 30–300: GLR over windows 4 and 6, EWMAs with alpha 0.1 and 0.2, one-sample Anderson–Darling on the last 16 and 24 points, the prefix Anderson–Darling recomputed at every step below 64; 13 channels.
