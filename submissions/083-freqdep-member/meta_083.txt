#37 with a second independent member: frequency and dependence channels.
---
The ensemble of #37 — augmented ranker, run-length suffix, twenty-four
trajectory networks, the mass battery as its own member — plus a second
member of the independent kind. A hundred channels the ensemble does not
otherwise read: rolling spectra on nine windows from 32 to 1024 points, each
against the history's band profile, and exponentially-weighted
autocorrelations at four lags over two windows, minus the history's. Alone
they are weak (0.5400) and almost uncorrelated with everything else (0.37);
under a slow, shallow classifier they add +0.0025 on fold 2 at a fifth of
the blend, on top of the mass member's +0.0038. Fold 2 reads 0.6232 against
0.6205 for #37. Streamed by the library's freqdep module, verified against
the training matrices to 1e-6.
