Nets trained on the triple boundary augmentation; the nets now outweigh the trees.
---
Six channel-trajectory networks trained on the original series plus the
triple boundary augmentation — three pseudo-series per original, each with
its break moved early. The data, not the architecture, was the lever: single
members reach 0.60 on the untouched fold, where no earlier member did, and
six of them replace the twenty-four-member pools entirely. Trees are the
augmented ranker (0.7) and the clean classifier (0.3); the blend is 0.45
trees to 0.55 nets, read off fold 2 at 0.6152 against 0.6106 for the
configuration shipped as #27.
